- Quotes are verbatim and at most 25 words.
- Every claim has a cite (source id, locator, quote).
- Do not claim finer than the source: dataset muscle groups are not muscle heads; hamstrings and calves have group statements.
- A source whose terms forbid AI use must not be added.
- If unsure, delete the claim.
- Structural tests do not prove a quote is real. Before accepting any quote (yours or a contributor's), check it is an exact
  substring of the source text (whitespace collapsed); a 2026-10-09 merge brought in 6 quotes that were not in the book.
- Source text lives outside the repo (Gray 1918: archive.org `anatomyofhumanbo00grayrich_djvu.txt`); re-download it to check.
